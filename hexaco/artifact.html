HEXACO-PI-R · 60 items

A portrait in six dimensions

The HEXACO self-report inventory measures six broad personality factors — including Honesty-Humility, the trait the Big Five leaves out. Rate 60 statements as you honestly are, and see where you fall against a college-sample norm.

Directions. Read each statement and decide how much you agree or disagree with it, describing yourself as you genuinely are — not as you'd like to be. There are no right answers.

Six factors

Each score is the mean of its ten items (1–5). The tick marks the norm-group average.

Reverse-keyed items are recoded before averaging, exactly per the HEXACO-60 scoring keys. Bands compare you to the selected group using its mean and standard deviation. Facet scales are short (2–3 items) and are rough indicators only, not reliable stand-alone measures.
