HEXACO-60 Personality Self-Test

The HEXACO-PI-R (60-item) self-report inventory — six broad personality factors.

Directions. Read each statement and decide how much you agree or disagree with it, describing yourself as you honestly are (not as you would like to be). Then pick a number.
1 strongly disagree 2 disagree 3 neutral 4 agree 5 strongly agree
0 / 60 answered

Scores are the mean of each factor's items (1–5) after reverse-keying, exactly per the HEXACO-60 scoring keys. The tick on each bar marks the norm-group average; the band label compares you to that group using its mean and standard deviation. Facet scales are very short (2–3 items) and are meant only as rough indicators, not reliable stand-alone measures.
